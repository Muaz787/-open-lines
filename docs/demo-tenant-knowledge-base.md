## What OpenLines is
OpenLines.ai is an AI phone receptionist for local service businesses. It answers your business calls 24/7, sounds like a friendly, professional human receptionist, and actually gets work done on the call — capturing and qualifying leads, booking and rescheduling appointments straight into your calendar, taking deposits, and texting or emailing confirmations. Every call is transcribed and summarized to your inbox. It's built and run by a Canadian company (Open Lines Technologies Inc.) and is privacy-compliant (PIPEDA).

**One-line pitch:** Never miss a call again — a 24/7 AI receptionist that answers, qualifies, books, and takes deposits, live in under 10 minutes.

## Who it's for
Local, appointment-driven and call-driven businesses, including: real estate, dental and medical/health clinics, beauty (salons, spas, barbershops), legal, plumbing/HVAC and home services, builders and contractors, restaurants, auto repair, and more. If you rely on the phone and can't afford to miss calls, it fits.

## What it does (capabilities)
- **Answers every call, 24/7** — days, nights, weekends, holidays, and when your line is busy. No more voicemail.
- **Captures and qualifies leads** — gets the caller's name, reason for calling, urgency, and the details you care about.
- **Books, reschedules, and cancels appointments** directly in your own calendar — Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, or Square Appointments.
- **Books with a specific team member** — "I'd like to see Sam" works; it matches your staff and books the right person.
- **Takes deposits on the call** — sends a secure Stripe or Square payment link by text so you cut no-shows. (Available on Pro and Business.)
- **Recognizes returning callers** — greets them by name and picks up where they left off.
- **Answers questions about your business** — hours, services, pricing, policies, directions — from your website and the documents you upload.
- **Sends confirmations and alerts** — SMS confirmation to the caller, plus a summary of every call to you by email, and optional SMS/WhatsApp owner alerts.
- **Gives you insights** — a dashboard with full transcripts, AI call summaries, and operations insights so you see what callers want and what you're missing.

## How it sounds
It speaks with a warm, natural voice and conversational timing — it isn't a robotic phone tree. For transparency (and privacy law), every call opens by letting the caller know they've reached a virtual receptionist and that the call may be recorded. It never pretends to be a specific human, and it never gives professional medical, legal, or financial advice — it schedules, takes messages, and answers general questions.

## Setup — live in under 10 minutes
1. Enter your website — the AI reads it and learns your business automatically.
2. Review what it found and fine-tune (name your receptionist, confirm your industry and hours).
3. Get your dedicated business phone number instantly.
4. Forward your calls (or use the new number) and you're live.
No technical skills, no hardware, no contracts.

## Pricing (CAD)
A 7-day free trial is included on every plan — no credit card required, and a business phone number is included from day one. Cancel any time. Annual billing saves roughly two months versus monthly.

- **Starter — $99/month** (or $990/year): 150 call minutes/month, 1 dedicated AI phone line, 24/7 answering, lead capture and qualification, appointment booking (Google & Outlook Calendar), booking with a specific team member, transcripts, AI summaries and call insights, SMS confirmations plus email/SMS/WhatsApp alerts, knowledge base (documents up to 10 MB / 50 pages, with scanned-document OCR).
- **Pro — $199/month** (or $1,990/year) — most popular: everything in Starter, plus deposit collection via Stripe & Square (including group and per-person deposits), HubSpot CRM sync, Slack notifications, and a larger knowledge base (25 MB / 300-page documents). 400 call minutes/month.
- **Business — $379/month** (or $3,790/year): everything in Pro, plus the largest knowledge base (50 MB / 1,000-page documents), priority support, dedicated onboarding, and early access to new features. 900 call minutes/month.

Extra minutes beyond your plan are billed at **$0.69/minute**. Prices are in Canadian dollars; applicable Canadian sales tax (GST/HST) is added at checkout.
